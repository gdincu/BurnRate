# BurnRate

**BurnRate** is a lightweight, aviation-inspired fuel management tool designed to help drivers determine the required fuel consumption needed to reach their destination. By comparing your current fuel state against your total trip requirements, **BurnRate** provides the real-time feedback necessary to ensure you reach your destination without running dry

## Features

* **Fuel State Calculation:** Quickly determine the maximum average fuel consumption ($L/100km$) you can afford for the remainder of your trip
* **Performance Monitoring:** Compares your current required consumption against a baseline (your original trip plan) to tell you if you are "Above" or "Below" target
* **Simple & Responsive:** Built with Bootstrap 5, the interface is clean, professional, and works on both desktop and mobile
* **Intuitive Feedback:** Uses color-coded visual indicators (badges and alerts) to provide instant status updates, similar to aviation fuel status checks

## How It Works

**BurnRate** performs a simple but critical check:

1.  **Input:** Enter your total tank capacity, total trip distance, distance covered so far, and your current fuel level percentage
2.  **Required Consumption:** The app calculates the consumption rate you *must* maintain to finish the journey
3.  **Baseline Comparison:** It compares this against the trip's baseline efficiency to let you know if you are driving efficiently or need to adjust your pace

## Getting Started

1.  Clone this repository or download the three files (`index.html`, `style.css`, and `script.js`).
2.  Open `index.html` in your favorite web browser.
3.  Input your trip details and click **Calculate** to check your status.
